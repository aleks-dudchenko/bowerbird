Connect to Bowerbird

This extension talks only to the Bowerbird app on your own machine. Pair once and it stays connected.

Looking for Bowerbird…
  1. Open Bowerbird and click Settings in the top right.
  2. Click Pair extension. A two-minute window opens.
  3. That is all — this page connects by itself the moment the window opens.
